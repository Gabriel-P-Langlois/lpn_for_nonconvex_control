Thanks for going through the experiments and for the three-way comparison. Replies to your four points below.

1. Names for the two routes.
I suggest we name them by mechanism rather than by authorship, since both routes deploy the same trained network psi_theta and differ only in how the prior is read out:
  - Route 1 -> "inversion readout" (in full: prior recovery by regularized inversion, citing Fang et al.). Its defining feature is the per-query solve of grad psi_theta(y) = x.
  - Route 2 -> "conjugate readout" (in full: prior recovery via a learned conjugate). Its defining feature is J(x) = G_theta(x) - (1/2)||x||^2 with G_theta approximating psi_theta^*, one forward pass.
  - The direct method keeps the name "direct fit".
For tables and legends: LPN-inv, LPN-conj, and Direct. Keeping the LPN stem signals the shared network and is fair to Fang et al.

2. Plots and metrics for the paper.
My current view, open to discussion:
  - From the synthetic families: the error-versus-dimension summary (validation MSE for psi and G, prior RMSE for both routes), plus the certificate columns (prox residual and Fenchel-Young gap). One representative per-family figure at most; the rest goes to supplementary material.
